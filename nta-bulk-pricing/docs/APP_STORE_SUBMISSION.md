## Positioning

- Final app name: TierWeave
- Category: Store design / Discounts
- One-line value proposition: Create product, variant, and collection quantity tiers that stay consistent from product page to checkout.
- Launch pricing: Free during launch

## Suggested listing copy

### Short description

Create clear quantity breaks for selected products, variants, or collections and publish them as a Shopify automatic discount.

### Key benefits

- Build fixed-price, percentage, or amount-off quantity tiers.
- Target individual products, variants, and collections.
- Preview and explicitly publish rule changes.
- Display matching quantity tiers on product pages.
- Keep pricing deterministic when multiple rules could match.

## Reviewer test flow

1. Install the app on the review store.
2. Open **Discount rules**.
3. Create a product rule with a minimum quantity and fixed-price tier.
4. Publish the rules.
5. Add the theme app block to the product template.
6. Add the qualifying quantity to cart.
7. Confirm the product-page tier and checkout discount agree.
8. Disable the rule and publish again; confirm the discount no longer applies.
